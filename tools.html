Geocaching toolbox

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Coordinate converter

Paste coordinates in any common format (ITM, Irish Grid and letter references work too), e.g. N 53° 21.123 W 006° 15.456, 53.3520, -6.2576 or 53 21 7.4 N 6 15 27.4 W, 715830 734697 or O 15904 34671.

Irish Grid & ITM

Convert between WGS84 (what your GPS and geocaching.com use), Irish Transverse Mercator (ITM) and the older Irish Grid (TM75), including letter references like O 15904 34671.

ITM is treated as equal to WGS84 (sub-metre). Irish Grid uses the official 7-parameter transformation, accurate to roughly 1 metre. Enough for caching, not for surveying.

Distance & bearing

Project a waypoint

Start from a point, then go a distance on a bearing to find the new coordinates (great for multi-stages).

Letter values & checksum
