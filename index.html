New to geocaching?

1. Get the basics

Geocaching is a real-world treasure hunt: use GPS coordinates to find hidden containers (caches), sign the logbook, and log your find online.

2. Play it right in Ireland

Ask permission where needed, avoid disturbing wildlife and archaeological sites, cache in, cache out, and leave no trace.
